## Fixed

- r2 — "[NIT:consistency] Ladder file as specified still has three controls". Accurate: D2's default
  is `len(Allowed) == 0`, so an omitted `control:` on a tool-less cell defaults back to control. D11
  now lists all three configs with their explicit flags — `e0-names control: true`,
  `e1-pack control: false, pack: true`, `e2-files control: false` — and states why the two `false`
  entries are mandatory, naming the `config.go:232–241` rejection and the zero-comparison-rows
  failure they otherwise cause.
- r2 — "[BLOCKING:design] `ladder pack`'s provenance write collides with the merge identity checks".
  Accurate: `MergeProvenance` (`provenance.go:385–397`) rejects a differing `LadderFile`,
  `LoomyardRepoSHA256`, `ServerName` or `RepsEffective`, and `Run` refuses a mismatched
  `RepsEffective` at `run.go:100–105`; a pack-only stub fails both on the first run. D4 now decides
  it explicitly: `ladder pack` calls `CollectInvocation` with the same inputs `Run` would use
  (`RepsEffective: l.Reps`, `SelectedCells:` every config id), then `MergeProvenance` and
  `WriteProvenance`, setting `KickstartPack` on the merged record after the merge returns. The
  accepted consequences are stated — one invocation that ran no reps, identifiable by its empty
  `server_hashes` and by `written_at == kickstart_pack.generated_at`, and a root pinned at `l.Reps`
  from the pack onward, which is correct under D9's locked n. The rejected alternative (a
  `MergeProvenance` exemption path) is recorded with its reason. `provenance_test.go` gains the
  pack-then-run merge as a regression.
- r2 — "[BLOCKING:design] The `pack_targets` validation rule is not implementable in `validate`".
  Accurate: `(*Ladder).validate()` has no repository root and touches no filesystem, so it cannot
  resolve a `card:` path. D11's rules are restated struct-level only, keyed on a new per-config
  `pack: bool` flag rather than on a card's contents: at most one `pack: true` per letter; a pack
  cell must declare a non-empty `card`; `pack_targets` non-empty iff some config sets `pack: true`;
  entries non-empty and unique. The sentinel/hash check moves to `ladder pack` (writing) and `run`'s
  pre-rep-1 verification (reading), both of which hold the root. D3 drops its `--card` flag as a
  consequence — the pack cell's own `card` is the only file the command can write, so the card
  written cannot disagree with the card the matrix renders. `config_test.go`'s bullet is rewritten to
  cover both directions of the iff and to assert `validate` still opens no file.
- r2 — "[BLOCKING:design] Gate accounting omits max-turns and unscored reps". Accurate:
  `summarize.go:236–282` excludes any rep whose `run.json` carries `scored: false`, split into
  `MaxTurnsCount` and `UnscoredCount`, and no `summary_matches` value exists for those. D8 now
  carries a four-disposition table with a three-valued gate column (`pass`/`fail`/`max_turns`/
  `unscored`), and predeclares: every rep that produced a transcript stays in the cost sample; a
  `max_turns` rep's `turns` is censored at the ceiling (identical across arms) while its `cost_usd`
  is not; > 2 of 10 censored in any arm makes `cost_usd` the primary comparison and is stated in the
  verdict line; > 2 of 10 `fail` flags that arm's cost numbers suspect; > 2 of 10 `unscored` flags
  its correctness accounting incomplete; both counts are reported per arm either way; and a rep
  discarded before producing a transcript is a harness event, not a disposition. `summarize_test.go`
  gains the mixed-disposition assertion.
- r2 — "[NIT:consistency] `main.go`'s header comment forbids a third subcommand". Accurate — the
  header ends "there is nothing left for a third subcommand to do". D3 now states that the header is
  rewritten to name three subcommands and to say why `pack` is one (it produces an artefact consumed
  by the prompt, not a step of the run loop — the distinction the original sentence was drawing), and
  that both usage strings (`main.go:21` and `:35`) gain `pack`.
- r2 — "[NIT:design] `ladder pack` and the run lock left unstated". Accurate. D3 step 1 now states
  that `ladder pack` acquires `AcquireRunLock(worktreeRoot, resultsRoot)` with a deferred release,
  exactly as `Run` does, and notes that a dead pack leaves the same operator-cleared stale lock a
  dead run does. Testing gains a `worktree_test.go` bullet for the held-lock failure and for release
  on both the success and the glyph-resolution-failure paths.
