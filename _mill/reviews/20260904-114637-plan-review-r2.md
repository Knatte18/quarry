### [BLOCKING:design] Card 5's hand check compares a repetition against a median
**Location:** batch 3 / card 5
**Issue:** The card counts turns, `toc` calls and cache-read for *one* repetition and requires comparison "against that cell's entry in the summary", declaring any mismatch a finding the conclusion must carry. `CellRecord.Metrics` holds `MetricStats` summarised over five repetitions, so a single repetition will disagree with the cell median in the ordinary case — the check as specified manufactures a false finding.
**Fix:** Scope the summary-side comparison to range containment (the repetition's value lies inside `cell_range`), and make `usage.json` the only value-for-value comparand.
