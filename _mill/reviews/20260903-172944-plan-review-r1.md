**Fix:** add `4` to batch 5's `depends-on` and `internal/ladder/gates.go` to cards 23 and 24 `Context:`.

### [NIT:decision] Scorer invocation is never specified by any card
**Demoted-from:** BLOCKING
**Location:** 06 card 26 (and 04 card 17, 08 card 34)
**Issue:** the discussion's scorer decision fixes a second `claude -p` call with `--tools ""`, `--max-turns 1` and the ladder file's `scorer.model`/`scorer.effort`; card 17 only builds the prompt and parses the reply, card 26 says "score" without naming a flag set or entry point, yet card 34 asserts "the scorer is invoked with a redacted answer".

### [BLOCKING:scope] Provenance reader, writer and field collection are unassigned
**Location:** 05 card 23, 06 card 26
**Issue:** batch 5's scope names "the provenance reader, merger and writer" but card 23's `Requirements:` names only `MergeProvenance`; no card names a read/write function, and nothing says who populates `quarry_commit`, `quarry_dirty`, `quarry_dirty_files`, `loomyard_repo_sha256`, `hostname`, `go_version`, `claude_version` or builds the `Invocation` — all of which need git and version calls through the runner seam.

**Demoted-from:** BLOCKING
**Location:** 00-overview `injectable-external-commands` / 05 cards 22, 24
**Issue:** the seam is fixed as `Run(ctx, dir, name string, args []string, stdin io.Reader, stdout io.Writer) error`, with no env and no stderr, while card 22 requires `BuildServer` to force `CGO_ENABLED=1` into the environment and card 24 asserts "the build helper passes the cgo variable through the runner seam"; the fake binary (card 33) also reports flag-assertion failures on stderr with no writer to capture them.
