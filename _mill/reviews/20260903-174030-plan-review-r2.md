## Findings

### [NIT:consistency] BuiltinTools declared in batch 6, used in batches 3 and 4
**Demoted-from:** BLOCKING
**Location:** 00-overview `### Decision: the-four-built-in-tools`; cards 26, 15, 19, 33
**Issue:** The decision requires every site to reference the `BuiltinTools` identifier declared in `run.go` (card 26, batch 6), but card 15 (batch 3, `prompt_test.go`) and card 19 (batch 4, `gates_test.go`) both need the four names and their batches depend only on [1] and [1,2,3] — the identifier does not exist yet, so those tests must re-spell the set, which is exactly what the decision forbids; card 33's fake binary is additionally constrained to "standard library only".
**Fix:** Declare the slice in a batch-1/batch-3 file (e.g. `config.go` or `prompt.go`) so every consuming site is downstream of it, and state how the fake binary receives the set.

### [BLOCKING:scope] Card 30 names ResolveQuarryRepoRoot without worktree.go in Context
**Location:** 07-summarize-report-cli, card 30
**Issue:** `Requirements:` explicitly calls `ResolveQuarryRepoRoot`, declared in `worktree.go` (card 20), but `Context:` lists only run.go, summarize.go, report.go, provenance.go and config.go — the implementer cannot read the function's signature.
**Fix:** Add `bench/loomyard-eval/ladder/internal/ladder/worktree.go` to card 30's `Context:`.

### [BLOCKING:scope] Cards 28 and 31 read the score record without score.go in Context
**Location:** 07-summarize-report-cli, cards 28 and 31
**Issue:** Both require reading a repetition's score record — its `scored` flag, its reason and its recall/precision values, all shapes fixed by `ScoreRecord` and the unscored stand-in helper in `score.go` (card 17) — yet neither card lists `score.go` in `Context:`, forcing cold-start exploration for the exact key names.
**Fix:** Add `bench/loomyard-eval/ladder/internal/ladder/score.go` to the `Context:` of cards 28 and 31.
